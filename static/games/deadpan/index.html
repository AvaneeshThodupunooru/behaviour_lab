HOW TO PLAY

Keep a straight face

  1. Enable your camera. Everything runs locally in your browser — video never leaves your device.
  2. Press Start Session in the controls drawer (☰, top-left). You get 46 seconds — the round ends and your result is sent automatically when the countdown hits zero.
  3. Watch the corner HUD. The ring shows your live laugh score; the number ticks up every time you're caught.
  4. Load stimulus. Add jokes, GIFs, images or clips to the deck in the drawer, then tap one to throw it fullscreen and try to break yourself (or a friend in the chair).
  5. A laugh only counts once per outburst — there's a short cooldown after each catch so one laugh doesn't rack up the score twice.
